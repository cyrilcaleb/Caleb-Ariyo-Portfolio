The project

What I was trying to make.

A short Bible story about Nehemiah rebuilding Jerusalem’s walls in the face of opposition. The edit moves from the broken state of the walls to rebuilding, criticism and eventual completion.

The challenge

The main creative challenge was finding visual scenes that matched each beat of the narration and kept the story understandable in a short runtime.

How I solved it

I generated AI-assisted scenes around the narration, selected and sequenced the visuals in CapCut, then used captions, music/sound and transitions to reinforce the progression from opposition to perseverance.
