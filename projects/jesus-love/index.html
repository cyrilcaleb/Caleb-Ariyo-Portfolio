The project

What I was trying to make.

A short-form Bible story presenting the crucifixion of Jesus through the theme of sacrificial love, moving from love and suffering toward redemption and hope.

The challenge

The challenge was creating visual scenes that could communicate the weight of the story while maintaining a consistent biblical atmosphere.

How I solved it

I generated and selected AI visual concepts to match the voiceover, then structured them in CapCut with captions, transitions, background sound and visual changes that move the viewer from love and sacrifice into suffering and finally toward hope.
