The project

What I was trying to make.

A long-form English lesson edited from raw client footage. The presenter teaches articles and examples from a whiteboard, with supporting visual elements added to make the lesson more engaging and easier to follow.

The challenge

The main challenge was keeping a lesson-based video engaging while maintaining the clarity and natural flow of the instructor’s teaching.

How I solved it

I refined the client’s raw footage in CapCut, shaping the pacing around the instructor’s explanations and adding supporting visual elements and CapCut sound effects at relevant moments to create more visual variety without losing the teaching flow.
