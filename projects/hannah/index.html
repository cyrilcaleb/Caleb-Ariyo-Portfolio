The project

What I was trying to make.

An emotional Bible storytelling short centered on Hannah, moving from pain and waiting toward hope. The edit relies on AI-generated visuals, voiceover and carefully paced scenes to carry the story.

The challenge

The challenge was creating the right visual scenes for the narration. Finding prompts that produced the exact imagery needed took iteration.

How I solved it

I generated the footage with AI, refined prompts with ChatGPT when the first results did not match the story, then assembled the sequence in CapCut. I added an ElevenLabs voiceover, CapCut background audio and transitions to shape the final flow.
