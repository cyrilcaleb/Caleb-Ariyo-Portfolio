The project

What I was trying to make.

A client-provided talking-head lesson turned into an interactive vocabulary quiz. The viewer is given time to think before each answer is revealed.

The challenge

The key challenge was balancing pace with participation: the video needed to move quickly enough for short-form while giving viewers enough time to process each question.

How I solved it

I cut unnecessary footage and pauses in CapCut and deliberately timed the answer reveal to roughly 5–7 seconds. I also used CapCut sound effects and the existing quiz graphics to reinforce the question-to-answer rhythm.
